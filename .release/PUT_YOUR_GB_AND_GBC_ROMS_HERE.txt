Place your Pokemon GEN 1 ROM files (.gb format) in this folder.

Compatible ROMs:
  - Pokemon Red (.gb)
  - Pokemon Blue (.gb)
  - Pokemon Yellow (.gb)

NOTE: Only Generation 1 Pokemon games are currently supported.
      Gen 2 (.gbc) and other generations are NOT yet supported.

Example:
  sdmc:/3ds/gen1recomp3ds/roms/pokemon_red.gb
  sdmc:/3ds/gen1recomp3ds/roms/pokemon_blue.gb
