Place your mods in this directory. Each mod is its own subfolder.

The 3DS reads mods from here before booting a game. Mods can be:
      * a .zip downloaded from a mod's GitHub releases (imported via the
            launcher), or
      * an already-extracted folder -- drop the mod's folder here directly
            (the 3DS can't unpack zips, so extract on a PC first).

A mod folder must contain a manifest.json at its root:
      sdmc:/3ds/gen1recomp3ds/mods/versaovermelha/manifest.json
      sdmc:/3ds/gen1recomp3ds/mods/versaovermelha/main.lua
      sdmc:/3ds/gen1recomp3ds/mods/versaovermelha/lang/...

NOTE: Mod support is a work in progress (WIP).
                  Not all mod features are functional yet.
